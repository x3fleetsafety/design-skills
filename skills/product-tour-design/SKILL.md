# Product Tour Design

Product tours are tempting. Used badly, they create tour fatigue. Modern preference: contextual help over forced tours.

## When to use

First sign-in for complex products. After feature launch on existing accounts. NOT as a substitute for clear UI.

## Max stops

5 stops, ideally 3. More = abandonment.

## Dismiss patterns

'Skip tour' always present. Esc dismisses. Don't pin behind a tour.

## Contextual help alternative

Persistent help icon next to complex features. Tooltip on hover. Doesn't force the tour but available when needed.

## Common mistakes

12 coachmarks on first login. Tour that blocks the actual product. Tour with no 'Skip'. Re-firing tour every session.
