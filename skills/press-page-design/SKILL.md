# Press Page Design

Press page works double-duty: helps journalists + serves as trust signal for prospects.

## Sections

Latest news (list of announcements + press releases). 'As seen in' logos. Press kit download (logos, brand guidelines, executive bios, screenshots). Media contact (email + name + phone).

## Logo downloads

Vector + raster. Light + dark backgrounds. Brand guidelines PDF. Make it self-serve for journalists.

## Executive bios

Photo + 100-word bio per leadership. Make sure photos are recent + professional.

## Press list

Reverse-chronological. Title + outlet + date + link. Don't pad with non-press items.

## Media contact

Real human email (not press@). Response within 24 hours expected.

## Common mistakes

Press page with no press (just plans). Outdated logos in 'as seen in' (defunct companies). No brand guidelines. press@ alias that nobody monitors.
