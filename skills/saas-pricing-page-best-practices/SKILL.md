# Saas Pricing Page Best Practices

The pricing page is the second-most-trafficked marketing page. Buyers in research mode spend serious time here. Make it work.

## Tier count

3 tiers = sweet spot. 4 tiers when you have a clear free + 3 paid. 5+ tiers when audience genuinely segments (Direct + Partner). More than 5 = decision paralysis.

## Featured tier

Always highlight one tier. The one you actually want most customers on (usually middle). Different border, slight elevation, different CTA color.

## 'Contact sales' tier

Acceptable for true enterprise (custom contracts, $50K+ ACV). NOT acceptable for sub-$10K ACV — hides pricing from prospects, signals you're either expensive or unsure.

## Annual toggle

Default to annual. Show savings explicitly. Display monthly equivalent. Smooth animation on price change.

## Below the table

FAQ specific to pricing concerns. Feature comparison matrix. ROI calculator. Customer logos. Final CTA repeated.

## Common mistakes

All tiers identical visually. Pricing only annual (no monthly). Custom enterprise with no anchor. No comparison context. Price jump cliffs between tiers.
