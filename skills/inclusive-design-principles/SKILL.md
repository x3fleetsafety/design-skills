# Inclusive Design Principles

Accessibility is the floor. Inclusion is the ceiling.

## Microsoft's inclusive design principles

Recognize exclusion (everyone is sometimes excluded). Solve for one, extend to many (curb cuts help wheelchairs AND parents AND skateboarders). Learn from diversity.

## Research with underrepresented users

Don't recruit only convenient samples. Pay users from disability communities, racial / gender minorities, non-English speakers.

## Design for different abilities

Color blindness (8% of men). Low vision (5% of population). Motor disabilities. Cognitive load.

## Voice + tone

Avoid idioms that exclude non-native speakers. Avoid jargon. Plain language helps everyone.

## Common mistakes

Designing for self / team. 'Edge case' framing for >5% of users. Accessibility consultation only at end. No paid disability research.
