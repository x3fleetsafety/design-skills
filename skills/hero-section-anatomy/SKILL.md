# Hero Section Anatomy

The hero is the most important section. It does 80% of the conversion work.

## Canonical structure (top to bottom)

1. **Eyebrow / badge** (optional) — "v1.0 live", "Now in beta", "Backed by Y Combinator"
2. **Headline (H1)** — one sentence, value-prop forward
3. **Subhead** — one paragraph explaining the value-prop and unique angle
4. **Primary CTA + secondary CTA** — one solid + one ghost
5. **Trust strip** — 4–6 inline trust signals (✓ no card, ✓ money-back, ✓ self-serve cancel)

Below that, optional: product screenshot, hero video, customer-logo bar.

## Headline patterns that work

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| Strikethrough anchor | "Stop paying ~~$130K~~. Hire the AI Safety Director for $79." |
| Outcome-led | "Cut your CSA score 30% in 12 months." |
| Pain-led | "Compliance shouldn't take your evenings." |
| Comparison | "What you'd hire from JJ Keller, but $79/month and 10x smarter." |

Avoid: "AI-powered platform for X" (generic). "Revolutionary compliance solution" (puffery).

## Subhead rules

- One sentence, max two
- Names what the product IS (not what it does)
- Names who it's for
- Avoids buzzwords

## Hero CTAs

- Primary CTA = solid, gradient or filled accent
- Secondary CTA = ghost / outline
- Same row, primary on left
- Both should be < 4 words

## Trust strip patterns

Inline checks with single short claims. Best at 4-6 items in a single row:
"✓ No card required · ✓ 30-day money-back · ✓ Self-serve cancel · ✓ Apache 2.0 corpus"

## Hero imagery decisions

| Approach | When to use |
|---|---|
| No image (text + CTA only) | When the brand is the product (AIPU, Notion early) |
| Product screenshot | When the UI is impressive at a glance |
| Abstract illustration | When the product is hard to show (AI/compliance/infra) |
| Hero video (no sound autoplay) | When motion tells the story (drag-and-drop, swipe) |
| Customer-logo bar | When you have 5+ recognizable customer brands |

## Common mistakes

- Headline + 4-sentence subhead → user gives up
- Three CTAs in hero → splits attention, halves conversion
- Hero video auto-playing sound → close-tab event
- Trust strip below the fold → wasted
- "Get started" CTA without context → user doesn't know what's next

## Where this fits in X3 Compass

X3 Compass hero uses strikethrough $130K → $79, gradient headline, one-paragraph subhead, primary + secondary CTAs, 6-item trust grid below.
