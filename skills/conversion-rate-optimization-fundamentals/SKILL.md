# Conversion Rate Optimization Fundamentals

Conversion isn't manipulation. It's removing friction between intent and outcome.

## The five conversion factors (in order of impact)

1. **Value clarity** — Does the user understand what this is in 5 seconds? Most "low converting" pages are unclear, not unpersuasive.
2. **Relevance to visitor** — Is this what THEY came for?
3. **Friction removal** — Form fields, required login, payment-up-front.
4. **Anxiety reduction** — Trust signals, money-back, "no card required."
5. **Distraction reduction** — Too many CTAs, too many options, too much copy.

Fix in this order.

## The LIFT framework

- **Value proposition** — primary driver
- **Relevance** — does the page match the ad/email/source?
- **Clarity** — can a 12-year-old summarize it?
- **Distraction** — what's pulling the user away from the CTA?
- **Anxiety** — what fears does the user have right now?
- **Urgency** — is there a real reason to act now?

## Above-the-fold

In the visible viewport on first load:
- One clear value proposition (headline)
- One sentence of supporting context
- One primary CTA
- One trust signal

Avoid multiple CTAs, hero video, three-paragraph subhead, every persona named.

## CTA design

- Highest visual contrast on the page
- First-person verb language ("Start my trial" not "Trial signup")
- ONE clear function
- Repeat at logical scroll points

## Form friction

For every field, ask: "Is this needed RIGHT NOW?" If not, defer.

A 4-field signup converts 60–80% better than 9-field.

## Trust signals that work

| Signal | When it works |
|---|---|
| Named customer logos | 3+ recognizable, with permission |
| Quote testimonials | Specific quotes with name + role |
| Numerical outcomes | Substantiated ("cut CSA 30% in 12 months") |
| Money-back guarantee | When you mean it |
| Security badges | When you actually have them |

## Anti-patterns (sleazy CRO)

- Fake countdowns
- Fake "X people just bought" notifications
- Pre-checked upsell checkboxes
- Hard-to-find cancel
- Forcing account creation to see pricing
- Hidden fees at checkout

These convert short-term and destroy LTV long-term.

## Common mistakes

- Designing hero before value prop is clear
- Three CTAs in hero — splits attention
- Trust signals only at the bottom of the page
- Pricing page without comparison context
- "Get started" without specifying what happens next
